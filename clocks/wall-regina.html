Regina

❦
Wood
Golden oak (recorded).
Movement
15-day double-spring movement on an iron bracket with Graham deadbeat escapement (same movement as the Moncton).
Dimensions
32.5 H x 16 W x 5 D in; 12 in metal dial.

1918 and 1928 catalogues only; octagon top with long drop; advertised as 'A mate to our Moncton'.
