Peterboro

Wood
Weathered oak / oak.
Movement
8-day time and strike; 1st ed gong + bell; 2nd ed cathedral gong (tuned-rod double-strike upgrade +$1).
Dimensions
11 H x 13 W x 6 D in.

1st ed 1913 (curved sides); 2nd ed 1913/1918; wartime brass-strip column variant; rare ebonized black finish recorded. Ebonized black 1st-edition variant: not one of the four traditional oak finishes advertised in the 1913/1918 catalogues; mottled white paint over black columns, presumed factory-applied. An example is owned by the Canadian Clock Museum; dial and movement marked ‘Berlin’ (manufacture 1916 or earlier).
