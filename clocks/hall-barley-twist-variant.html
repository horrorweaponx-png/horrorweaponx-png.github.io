Barley Twist Variant

Wood
Oak (from recorded examples).

Not documented.
Dimensions
Not documented.

A clock never listed in any Pequegnat catalogue, recognizable by its barley-twist columns — judged the most beautiful and substantial of all Pequegnat grandfather clocks. Very few known (possibly six). Expensive when they surface.
