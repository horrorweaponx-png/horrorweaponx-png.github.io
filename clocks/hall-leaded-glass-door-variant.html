Leaded Glass Door Variant

❦
Wood
Oak, in the factory’s usual style.
Movement
Berlin 8-day weighted movement chiming on two tuned rods.
Dimensions
79.5 H x 18.5 W x 13.5 D in.

An uncatalogued variant clock with a leaded glass door, oak case in the factory’s usual style, and Pequegnat-style elongated dial numerals — judged a genuine factory variant, possibly a special order or small run.
