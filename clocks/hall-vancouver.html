Vancouver

❦
Wood
Oak (from recorded example).
Movement
8-day time and strike on traditional gong; label and movement signed Berlin.
Dimensions
81 H x 22.5 W x 14.5 D in at the top.

The Vancouver hall clock from the 1913 and 1918 catalogues, with both the label and movement signed Berlin and the traditional gong striking the hours and half hours. Rare and one of the more difficult Pequegnat hall clocks to find.
