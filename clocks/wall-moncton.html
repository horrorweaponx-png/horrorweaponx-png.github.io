Moncton

❦
Wood
Quarter-sawn oak (golden/dark); mahogany recorded.
Movement
15-day time-only, double spring, Graham deadbeat escapement.
Dimensions
Long drop: 35-35.5 H x 15.5-16 W x 4.75-5 D in; short drop: 32.5-33.25 H in.

Long-drop and short-drop cases; railway-quality station clock (CNR/CPR); 1913, 1918, 1920-25, 1928 catalogues; levellers and beat indicator.
