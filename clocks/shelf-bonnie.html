Bonnie

❦
Wood
Oak (recorded example).
Movement
8-day time and strike with gong.
Dimensions
10.625 H in; widths 9.75 (straight base) / 11.5 (beveled base) / 9.25 (curved base); depth ~4.25-4.5 in.

Three case designs (straight, beveled, curved base); 1928 catalogue only; factory converted some from Dominion (decal dial found under the bezel dial).
