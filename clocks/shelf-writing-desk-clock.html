Writing Desk Clock

❦
Wood
Oak (from recorded example).
Movement
8-day time and strike on gong.
Dimensions
Not documented.

An unusual clock owned by Robert Russell in Chatham, Ontario: a writing desk with an 8-day Pequegnat movement and gong. As far as is known, certainly one of a kind.
