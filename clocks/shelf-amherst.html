Amherst

Wood
Quarter-sawn oak (recorded example).
Movement
8-day time and strike; crown version strikes a pair of tuned rods, no-crown version a single rod.
Dimensions
11.5 H x 16.75 W x 7 D in (crown); 10.5 H x 16.75 W x 7 D in (no crown).

Two versions: with and without the catalogued crown piece; shown in the 1928 catalogue.
