Nelson

❦
Wood
Mahogany.
Movement
8-day weighted movement with brass weights; double strike on tuned rods for hour and half hour.
Dimensions
Recorded 81 H x 18.5 W x 13 D in (Skip notes full-size 81 H x 22.5 W x 13 D).

Sunken dial; extremely rare, few known examples.
