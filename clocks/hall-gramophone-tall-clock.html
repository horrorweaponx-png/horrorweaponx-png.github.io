Gramophone Tall Clock

❦
Wood
Mahogany (from recorded example).
Movement
Clock movement not documented; built-in gramophone in the lower case.
Dimensions
Not documented.

A most interesting one-of-a-kind clock: it has a built-in gramophone and was built for a Pequegnat family member. Photograph courtesy of Wes Playter of Newmarket, Ontario; his father Glenn owns the clock.
