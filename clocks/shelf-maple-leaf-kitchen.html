Maple Leaf Kitchen

❦
Wood
Oak (gingerbread pressed case).
Movement
8-day with gong; maple-leaf pendulum.
Dimensions
1st ed gingerbread: 22.5 H x 15.25 W in; 2nd ed: 22.5 H x 12.5 W in.

1st ed (1904 only): Fan Top, Points Up, Points Down - the Points Down is the only catalogued mantel clock with the escape wheel outside the front plate; 2nd ed Pointed/Round Top (1913, 1918).
