King Edward

❦
Wood
Quarter-sawn oak; walnut recorded.
Movement
8-day Time Only or Time and Strike (single tuned rod recorded); calendar recorded on 2nd ed; 15-day movement variant documented.
Dimensions
Time-only: 30 H x 16 W in; Time/Strike: 29 H x 16 W x 4.5-5.25 D in.

1904 supplement and every catalogue after; King Edward VII decal; 1st ed brass sash + cardboard dial, 2nd ed wooden frame. 1st Edition: brass sash over the dial, Time Only or Time and Strike, no calendar. 2nd Edition: wooden hinged frame, offered as Time Only, Time and Strike, Time and Strike and Calendar, or Time and Calendar.
