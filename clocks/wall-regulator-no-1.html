Regulator No. 1

❦
Wood
Mahogany or golden oak.
Movement
8-day WEIGHT-DRIVEN (brass weights on iron bracket), TIME ONLY, 80 beats/min; Graham deadbeat escapement, lantern pinions, maintaining power.
Dimensions
36 H x 15.75 W in; 12 in dial.

1904 supplement and every catalogue after; advertised as 'The Finest Office Clock Made'; the most accurate clock Pequegnat made.
