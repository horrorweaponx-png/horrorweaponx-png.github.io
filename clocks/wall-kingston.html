Kingston

❦
Wood
Oak, Arts and Crafts style.
Movement
8-day time and strike.
Dimensions
32 H x 15 W x 4.25 D in; 11.5 in chapter ring with brass numbers.

An Arts and Crafts style clock very similar to the Ottawa, differing mainly in its square dial (the Ottawa has the round dial). First featured in the 1904 catalogue #1 supplement, then the 1913 and 1928 catalogues; despite fewer catalogue appearances it is easier to find than the Ottawa.
