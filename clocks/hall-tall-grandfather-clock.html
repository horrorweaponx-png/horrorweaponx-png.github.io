Not documented.

Not documented.

The 'biggest ever' Pequegnat grandfather clock, made for Ralph Pequegnat of Port Elgin, Ontario. It stands 8 ft 3 in tall — the center finial is removed because it will not fit an 8-foot ceiling. One of a kind. Photograph courtesy of Jim Connell.
