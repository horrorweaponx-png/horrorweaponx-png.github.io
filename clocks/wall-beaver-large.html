Beaver (Large)

❦
Wood
Mahogany or oak.
Movement
8-day time and strike on tuned rods; 5 in porcelain dial.
Dimensions
35.25 H x 14 W x 7 D in.

1st Issue; 1918 catalogue; composite beaver topper - Pequegnat's hallmark Canada-tribute clock; rare walnut 1st-issue example with porcelain dial recorded. Two pendulum-bob sizes seen: the large bob is typical of the 1st issue, the small bob likely transitional toward the 2nd.
