Leader

❦
Wood
Oak (museum; weathered oak recorded); mahogany recorded.
Movement
8-day; hours and half hour on the traditional gong.
Dimensions
Oak: 76 H x 22.5 W x 15 D in; mahogany: 81.5 H x 21 W x 14 D in (recorded examples).

Leaded glass door with etched-glass border on recorded example; one of the easier hall clocks to find.
